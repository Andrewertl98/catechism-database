# Review packet — Dogmas And Doctrine

**Questions in this packet:** 232 (levels 1–23)

## What we are asking

Please check each question below for **doctrinal accuracy** and for whether the
**citation actually supports** what the question and explanation claim. A question
can be factually true and still be wrong for our purposes if the citation does not
carry the claim.

For each question, tick one of the three verdicts and add a note where useful.
Anything you mark Rejected or Approved-with-change will be revised and sent back
to you — nothing goes into the app on a maybe.

## What happens to your answers

Only questions you approve get marked audited and served in the app. The six
doctrinally load-bearing topics are hard-gated in code: their harder levels are
literally not shown to any user until your sign-off is recorded. That gate is
enforced by a signature check in the app, not by a flag in a file, so it cannot be
set by accident.

Please reply to the email this came attached to with your verdicts — a written
reply from you is the record of the review, and it gets archived alongside the
content. The technical signature only proves the repository owner marked something
audited; your email is what proves a theologian actually read it.

### 1. `dogmas-and-doctrine-l1-001`  ·  Level 1  ·  Multiple choice

**Question.** A 'dogma' is best defined as:

**Answer options.**
- [✓] A truth revealed by God and definitively proposed by the Church as binding on every believer
- [ ] Any opinion a bishop happens to hold personally
- [ ] A purely disciplinary rule, like a fasting requirement, that can change over time
- [ ] A theory theologians debate but the Church has never formally decided

**Explanation shown to the user.** A dogma is not a private opinion or a changeable rule -- it is a truth God has revealed, which the Church's Magisterium has definitively taught, and which every Catholic is bound to believe.

**Hint.** Think about what makes a dogma different from a rule that could someday change.

### 2. `dogmas-and-doctrine-l1-002`  ·  Level 1  ·  True / false

**Question.** True or False: Every single teaching the Church holds is a dogma.

**Correct answer.** False

**Explanation shown to the user.** False. 'Doctrine' is the wider category -- the Church's whole body of teaching, some of it solemnly defined as dogma, some of it taught with real authority but not (yet, or ever) formally defined. Disciplinary rules, like the Friday abstinence requirement, are a third, changeable category again.

**Hint.** Consider whether 'dogma' and 'doctrine' are simply two names for the exact same thing.
